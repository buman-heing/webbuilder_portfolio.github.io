Our Mission

Zambia's Gateway to World-Class Higher Education

ZISD — the Zambian Institute for Sustainable Development — is a youth-led NGO based in Lusaka. We believe that poverty is not only physical but mental, and that every brilliant Zambian student deserves a path to the world's best universities, regardless of their background.

As the first Kaplan Certified Education Provider in Southern Africa and third on the continent, our alumni have gone on to study at institutions including Penn State, Harvard, and leading universities across the US and Canada — many on full-tuition scholarships.
